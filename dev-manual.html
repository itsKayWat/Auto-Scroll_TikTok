Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 14, 2024

Introduction

Auto Scroll for TikTok is a browser extension designed to automatically scroll to the next video when the current one ends, enhancing the user experience by providing seamless video playback.

Data Collection

This extension does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. It operates entirely on the user's device and does not transmit any data to external servers.

Data Usage

Since no data is collected, there is no data usage to disclose.

Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties.

User Rights

As no data is collected, users have no data-related rights to exercise.

Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please reach out to us on @ll_kayawt_ll.
